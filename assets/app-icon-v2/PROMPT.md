## Generation prompt

+Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: finished macOS application icon for Margin Notes, a quiet local notebook that slides out from the screen edge.
Primary request: Create one beautifully crafted, memorable app icon with a strong silhouette and restrained native macOS depth. A warm ivory paper note with a clearly defined sage-green vertical margin, on a deep forest-green rounded-square tile. The paper is softly rounded and slightly dimensional, with three broad forest-green text strokes of different lengths. A small folded paper corner gives a tactile detail. The vertical margin is the distinctive visual idea, reflecting the app name and its screen-edge notebook workflow.
Style/medium: precise premium digital icon design, clean geometry, softly bevelled ceramic-like tile, paper-like note, subtle soft shadows and restrained highlights. Calm, warm, contemporary, exceptionally simple and readable at small sizes.
Composition/framing: square 1024x1024 canvas, straight-on view, a single centered macOS rounded-square tile with approximately 8% clear padding around it; rounded corners and genuinely transparent outer area. Note symbol occupies approximately two thirds of the tile. All elements large and bold enough to read at 32 pixels. Balanced spacing and deliberate negative space.
Color palette: existing app colors, deep forest green #385747, warm cream #FDFCF8, sage #A8BCA0. Gentle tonal variation within these colors.
Text: no letters, no words, no numbers. The only text-like shapes are three short broad rounded horizontal strokes.
Constraints: one isolated icon only, transparent outside the tile, no scene, no mockup, no framing labels, no border around the canvas, no extra icons, no pencils, no badges, no watermark, no glossy glare, no glass effect, no excessive tiny details.
