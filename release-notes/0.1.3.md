**Match vault** now wears your vault's colours exactly as Obsidian shows them. In light mode, folder icons, Markdown headings, links and folder dots had been softened toward the text colour; they are now fully colourful, as in dark mode. The Saturation slider has been removed.

Notes no longer need a title. Write straight into the body and the card opens on your writing; menus, history and Markdown exports name an untitled note by its first line.

Drag a folder onto the middle of another folder tab to move it inside, or onto a tab's edge to place it beside. Drop a subfolder on the top **All** tab to make it a top-level folder.

Turn on **Preferences → Your workspace → Launch at startup** to open Margin when you log in.

The CXTasks Glass theme is now simply **Glass**.

This update is signed and notarized for Apple Silicon Macs. Existing users can install it through **Check for Updates…** in Margin's menu.
