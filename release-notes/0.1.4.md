**Document and task links are now chips.** In a note's reading view, a link to a local document shows the icon of the app that opens it (Onyx, or Obsidian) and the document's own title, like a smart chip in Google Docs. A pasted path shows the document's title; a link you labelled keeps your label. A document that has moved shows a faded, dashed chip, so you see it before you click.

With CXTasks links turned on, `T42` appears as a chip with the CXTasks icon, the task's number and its title. Finished tasks are struck through; tasks in Trash or the archive are faded. Margin reads these from CXTasks without changing anything.

Chips refresh whenever the panel regains focus. Notes are still saved as plain Markdown, and links look as before while you edit.

This update is signed and notarized for Apple Silicon Macs. Existing users can install it through **Check for Updates…** in Margin's menu.
